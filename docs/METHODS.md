# AHAS V1 method contract

AHAS measures supplied text and timestamps. No reported distance is an authorship probability, and no numerical threshold establishes an underlying cause. This document describes registered methods; each result's module status says whether they actually ran. Engineering test outcomes belong in `EVALUATION.md`.

The machine-readable authority is `src/account_history_analyzer/registry.py`. `registry_document()` returns explicit descriptions, formulas, units, denominators, missingness rules, evidence rules, versions, origins, and source-reference identifiers. Resource and registry content hashes belong in canonical results. Registry membership is not an execution claim.

## Text, denominators, and missingness

The original source string is retained as evidence. Each derived record exports its ordered transformation identifiers. Analysis views normalize line endings to LF and Unicode to NFC. CommonMark mode removes explicit quotes, code, images, raw HTML tokens and defined URL/handle spans. Plain mode does not interpret Markdown delimiters. Paragraphs, records, and excluded spans create hard boundaries. Pure formatting around retained text and descriptive link labels do not create boundaries. Line/block provenance and normalized segment offsets are distinct; normalized offsets must not be described as offsets into the raw source.

The operational regex is `(?u)[^\W\d_]+(?:['’][^\W\d_]+)*|\d+`. All-digit matches are number tokens; the others are word tokens. Hyphens and underscores split words. Straight and curly internal apostrophes are preserved for surface counts and normalized for case-insensitive lexical matching. NFC composes where Unicode defines a composition; remaining combining marks can separate matches. Python Unicode `\d`, `\w`, and `str.isdigit()` are operational classifications and do not equal a universal linguistic definition of words/numbers. Emoji do not form lexical word tokens. Behavior and the Python/Unicode version are part of the environment contract.

Style word counts exclude number tokens. Reuse tokens include both words and numbers. Retained code-point counts include punctuation and whitespace within retained segments. Word-character totals count alphabetic code points in word tokens, excluding apostrophes. Uppercase fraction is `isupper_count / (isupper_count + islower_count)`; uncased characters do not enter its denominator. Exact standalone `i`/`I` is a literal descriptor, not confirmed pronoun use.

Every literal punctuation category has its own count. Runs of at least three ASCII periods overlap literal period counts. The overlap is intentional and the two must not be summed as disjoint categories. Rates are pooled numerator sums divided by pooled denominator sums. Quantiles use linear interpolation at `(n−1)q`, equivalent to NumPy `quantile(method="linear")`. An empty denominator produces null and a reason; it never produces a perfect comparison or reassuring zero.

Contraction alternatives are exact within-segment token matches. Both counts are retained even at zero. The display fraction requires at least ten matched opportunities by default; a nonempty arithmetic fraction below that guard remains evidence with `insufficient_opportunities`. Contextual interchangeability is not established.

## Comparisons and temporal analysis

English-specific profiles require declared `en`; language is never inferred. Primary body windows require present usable English text, a creation timestamp, at least twenty words per record, and selected kind/community membership. Comments and submission bodies form separate streams; titles are separate measurements. Accumulate whole records until both 1,000 words and eight records are reached. Final short remainders remain visible and are excluded from primary segmentation. Large single-record shares above 0.50 are flagged.

Character comparisons keep representation and n-gram lengths separate. Function-word distributions include an exhaustive `OTHER_WORD` category. Delta frequencies and reference parameters must use `per_1000_word_tokens`; missing references yield `not_run_missing_reference`. The numerical implementation evaluates the algebraically identical `abs(f_A − f_B) / sigma` coordinate contribution to avoid cancellation when reference means are large; required means and reference provenance are still validated. The shipped toy reference requires an explicit override and the `toy_reference` limitation. Zero representation distance establishes identity only in that representation.

For each complete stream, the temporal surface family is comma, period, question mark, exclamation mark, semicolon, colon, straight/curly apostrophe, em dash, and ellipsis rates per 1,000 words, plus uppercase fraction and average word length. The function family contains individual fixed function-word rates, without the dependent `OTHER_WORD` residual. Features missing in any window or with population SD ≤ 1e-12 are excluded and logged. Retained coordinates are standardized with population SD (`ddof=0`), divided by the square root of their retained family size, then by the square root of the number of nonempty families. Save the order and scale parameters.

PELT uses L2 segment cost, `min_size=3`, `jump=1`, at least eight qualified windows, and penalty `lambda*ln(N)` per internal change. Lambda 1.0 is primary; 0.5, 2.0, and 4.0 are fixed sensitivity settings. The objective excludes the terminal endpoint from the internal boundary count. Constant/all-excluded vectors yield `no_measurable_variation`. Account-local scaling can change when history is added.

Production uses the pinned `ruptures.costs.CostL2` numerical primitive and the verbatim `Pelt._seg` method from [PR #383 commit a28574d9e63b0c2a966e176a1d049d3c9deaaaaf](https://github.com/deepcharles/ruptures/commit/a28574d9e63b0c2a966e176a1d049d3c9deaaaaf), wrapped in a private subclass of ruptures 1.1.10. Its optimizer identifier is `ruptures_pelt_pr383_a28574d_v1`. A dominated candidate expires only when the witness split can start a legal following segment. The upstream method uses strict comparisons and sums complete partition costs; it has no local pruning slack. Equal candidate totals choose the first eligible start in increasing endpoint order. AHAS validates finite inputs/arithmetic and independently exports SSE plus beta times internal boundaries, removing the upstream per-segment convention's constant extra beta. The window guards, family scaling and penalty defaults are unchanged. Exact or near ties can differ from the previous AHAS accumulation; objective oracle checks and deterministic reruns remain required. This correctness result supplies no classification accuracy or calibrated penalty interpretation. See [PR383.md](PR383.md) for provenance and actual validation.

Same-window sensitivity uses maximum one-to-one matching within ±1 window; ties minimize total displacement then lexicographic pairs. Different constructions compare open record-order boundary-interval interiors, not window indices; touching endpoints and separated intervals are reported separately. Window targets 500 and 2,000, within-community streams, known-edit exclusions, and exact repeat-reduced views are predefined. Alternate constructions rerun pooled kinds; primary within-community analyses provide the separate community controls without a Cartesian parameter expansion. Skipped insufficient settings are omitted from the executed-setting denominator; an executable constant-vector setting counts as a measurement with no boundaries. The result is parameter stability, never confidence.

## Reuse, timing, and evidence

Raw strings, normalized prose segment sequences, and normalized token sequences with boundaries are separate equivalence relations. Empty/sentinel text is excluded. Exact phrases below twenty words are not substantial-reuse headlines. Five-token shingle sets use exact tuple equality. Jaccard is intersection/union, and directed containment is intersection/source-set size; empty sets abstain. Decisions compare integer products with rational thresholds (default 80/100 and 90/100). Shingle sharing is not necessarily one contiguous shared passage. A claimed contiguous passage must exist in both cited segments. Connected groups preserve edges and do not assert that every pair meets a threshold.

Repeat reduction keeps earliest exact-prose representatives. Optional near reduction greedily compares with retained representatives, picks greatest similarity then earliest representative, and exports every exclusion. It changes only the sensitivity view. Candidate-pair exhaustion preserves exact matches and produces an explicit incomplete/resource-limit result.

Activity includes all distinct supplied events with creation times, including removed/deleted text. Integer-microsecond differences retain simultaneous events. Daily/hourly/weekday bins use UTC. First/last calendar days and supplied coverage gaps require visibility. Coefficient of variation uses population SD/mean and abstains for fewer than two intervals or zero mean. Sliding windows are inclusive. `greedy_nonoverlapping_fixed_window_bursts` anchors at the earliest remaining event, chooses the farthest event within duration, emits if its event-count guard holds, then advances past the group; otherwise it advances one event.

Link hosts are parsed locally, lowercased, IDNA-normalized where supported, and stripped of a final DNS dot. `www` is distinct and no public-suffix grouping occurs. Occurrences and records-with-host have different denominators. Parent differences use supplied times and are called creation-to-parent-creation seconds. Local cycles must terminate safely. Neither links nor parent context is fetched.

Feature explanations sort by descending unrounded value then feature ID. At most two qualifying highest-rate records per side use chronological/ID ties and a minimum denominator; ordinary context comes from a record nearest median length. Selected examples do not represent the whole window. Changed and stable features should both be visible. Source IDs, feature values and actual evidence objects support findings; fixture names, account IDs, truth labels and boundary annotations never enter feature calculations.

## Reproducibility and validation

Static reports render fixed templates and inline deterministic SVG from exported result/window measurements. Chart metadata retains exact plotted values; missing values break trajectories. Source text is escaped as text, HTML has no scripts or remote assets, and source/evidence links use digest-derived internal anchors. Omitting excerpts also omits source-derived n-gram example strings; it preserves numerical measurements and still exposes potentially identifying metadata. Raw-source snippets and normalized-segment snippets retain distinct offset labels.

Canonical JSON uses UTF-8, sorted keys, compact separators, finite unrounded values, positive zero, and a final newline. Canonical records are expanded, deduplicated and sorted by missing-time flag, integer timestamp, then ID. Input row order, input file paths, current clock, local timezone, and Python hash randomization are not analytical inputs. Operational receipts contain raw-file hashes, timing, paths and actual environment details separately. Reference paths become content identities in analytical configuration.

Independent numerical comparisons use stated test tolerances (cosine absolute 2e-15; Jensen–Shannon absolute 1e-12). These are cross-implementation agreement tolerances, not decision cutoffs. Tiny mathematically impossible negative/out-of-range excursions are clamped only within 1e-12; meaningful in-range distances are not rounded away. Byte identity is an environment-specific tested claim. No universal cross-platform claim follows from matching hashes once. Numerical oracles and synthetic signal tests do not establish real-world validation. Evaluation truth is read only by evaluation tooling. Held-out labels must not choose preprocessing, references, vocabulary or thresholds. The scientific status remains `real_world_validation: not_established` until independent, appropriate external evaluation exists.

The evaluator-only `evaluation_metrics.py` names `different_author` as the positive paired-text label and treats larger distances as positive rankings. ROC AUC counts positive–negative wins with half credit for equal scores. Average precision sums precision times recall increments after including an entire equal-score group; it does not interpolate or break score ties by row order. Decisions use only an explicitly supplied frozen threshold with `score >= threshold`. Missing scores abstain; every ratio exports its numerator and observed denominator, with null values and reason codes when undefined. These metrics report an external task's supplied labels, not an account-level probability.

External stream labels use canonical original-record split coordinates: split `k` precedes the record at zero-based ordinal `k`. A production boundary spanning original record positions `[a,b]` becomes the inclusive candidate split interval `[a+1,b]`. Evaluator matching uses an explicitly preregistered integer tolerance, maximizes one-to-one matches, minimizes total point-to-interval displacement, then applies lexicographic coordinate ties. Location error is zero inside an uncertainty interval and otherwise distance to its nearest endpoint; it does not infer an exact transition location inside the interval. Boundary precision, recall, location error and false candidates per executed unchanged stream expose their denominators, together with abstained-stream coverage. No thresholds, confidence intervals or acceptance accuracy targets are learned by these arithmetic helpers.
