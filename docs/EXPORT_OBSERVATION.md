## Interpretation

AHAS `status=present` means a body is supplied in the analytical snapshot. It
does not assert public visibility, original contribution completeness, wording
at creation time, personal authorship or source authenticity. The new policy
preserves those unknowns in AHIF and its receipt. Literal source fields are
measured under an explicit frozen CommonMark interpretation; the source dialect
is not claimed equivalent. Titles stay separate; blank bodies are not invented.

Known removed/deleted/restricted writing and recognized unsupported markup are
excluded from text measurements under this version. Otherwise usable posting
events remain in activity. Unknown language stays `und`; generic counts and
reuse do not require English. Explicit scope-bound supplier language declarations
are optional and were not supplied for the real regression.

The frozen renderer omits arbitrary source/coverage notes. The companion produces
a separate context-bearing HTML/Markdown presentation with a receipt binding
the original and derived reports. The original canonical analysis remains the
target of `ahas verify --recompute`. Present the interpretation context alongside
results; a bare engine report is not the full reviewed projection presentation.
