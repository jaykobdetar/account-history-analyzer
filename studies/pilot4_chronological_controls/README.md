# Pilot 4: chronological controls — feasibility stop

**No eligible account blocks survived the frozen post-audit construction rules. No corpus chronological analysis was run.** Candidate occurrence, switch localization, attainable window resolution, abstention rates and replay agreement are all unmeasured. They must not be read as zero performance or zero abstention.

The intended evaluation used unchanged AHAS 1.0.4 full chronological analysis on matched source-account continuity controls and constructed source-account switches, both with and without a community change. The method fixed whole-comment date and volume matching, a 10-record localization tolerance, all four experimental conditions, and separate occurrence, localization, resolution and availability outcomes before any corpus chronology scores.

Score-free feasibility evaluated **505 registered pair/calendar combinations**: 303 across the three original pairs, followed by 202 across two explicitly authorized new pairs. Original matched-block capacities were 0/1/0; each new pair had capacity one. The frozen allocation retained seven distinct candidate accounts and reserved three blocks against a five-block ceiling. Auditing removed 195 of 2,447 candidate records. After the unchanged final rules, the retained strata had 1/0/1 individually qualified accounts, no compatible account-pair edges, and **zero selected blocks or constructed cases**. The final block deficits remain 1/2/2. These are results for this fixed construction and candidate pool, not proof that every possible chronological design is infeasible.
