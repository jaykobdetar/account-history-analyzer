## Intended comparison and fixed controls

Every selected block would contain two source accounts, two discussion communities, and eight early/late source cells. Four early anchors would each supply the following four conditions, yielding 16 related histories per block:

| Condition | Late source account | Late community |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Continuity, same community | Same | Same |
| Constructed switch, same community | Other | Same |
| Continuity, changed community | Same | Other |
| Constructed switch, changed community | Other | Other |

The fixed shared cut divides adjacent 180-day bands. Records must be whole comments with 20–500 frozen retained words. The nearest-cut final prefix must reach both 5,000 words and 40 records, within 5,500 words and 200 records. Across the eight cells, maximum/minimum word and record ratios must be at most 11/10 and 5/4; the four median dates within each period must span at most 30 days. Exact rational matching maximizes disjoint block count, then minimizes the registered metadata cost, then applies the frozen identity-hash tie order. Original IDs, text, dates, community and thread metadata are preserved; only the common account alias of an eventual constructed input would change.

All 57 earlier protected/excluded identities and all 60 pilot3-scored identities are excluded. Account labels are source identifiers, not verified human authors. A constructed source-account switch does not represent a verified takeover, and a continuity control does not imply constant writing style. The full ordinary analyzer was planned for sampled constructed snapshots, not complete real account histories.

## Candidate pool and available-content audit

Preparation retained nearest-cut buffers targeting 8,000 words and 64 records per cell, capped at 8,500 words and 300 records. The [actual pool](../inventory/candidate-preparation-01.json) contained **2,447 records and 220,065 retained words from seven accounts**. Source extraction read 12,368,568 rows and 7,511,987,617 uncompressed bytes across six archives, within the fixed 15-million-row and 10-billion-byte bounds. It preserved original source lines and their hashes privately and computed no style scores.

The unchanged prior audit wrapper and leakage engine used exact, near-reuse, template, recognized quotation and original-thread relations. Historical protection included the previous 22 files plus the original 5,773 selected pilot3 records. Candidate members connected to historical material or multiple candidate cells were purged symmetrically. No later source refill was allowed.

The [audit completed](../inventory/content-audit-01.json) with 532,004 broad candidate pairs, exactly matching the independently counted engine-eligible set and remaining below the two-million-pair ceiling. It reported 99 exact and 2,559 near relations, no template or recognized quotation relations, and **195 purged / 2,252 surviving candidate records**. Purge reasons were 21 content cross-cell records, 165 thread cross-cell records and nine records crossing the historical thread boundary. These relation counts describe the complete audit graph, not a count of independent new-account events.

The available-content and grouping audit passed. Independence of the full historical scope remains unknown: 17 historical bodies were unavailable, and unobserved content relationships, unmarked quotations and semantic paraphrases are outside the claimed coverage. Audit completion therefore did not certify unrestricted independence or establish anything about authorship, automation or deception.
