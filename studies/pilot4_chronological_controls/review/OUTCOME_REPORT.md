## Evidence and limits

The [independent prepared-input check](prepared-independent-check-01.json) verified all 2,447 candidate source hashes against upstream metadata, 2,290,911 original source-copy bytes, audit purges, exclusions and exact post-audit selection. It reproduced zero blocks and zero cases, with no preprocessing sample because there were no final selected records. The [terminal identity check](../environment/terminal-identity-verification.json) confirmed the frozen package and configuration. Earlier failed intake and evaluation-check attempts are retained, with corrections and timing described in the [methods report](METHODS_AND_PREPARATION.md).

This result does not answer whether source-account switches are distinguishable from continuity or community changes. It applies to the registered archives, dates, pool, audit and matching rules. The supplied corpus is sampled, account identities are unverified proxies, and 17 historical bodies were unavailable to the content audit. It supports no claim about actual takeovers, human authorship or bots.

A new registered attempt would need at least one auditable two-account block with all eight account/community/period cells meeting the same post-audit controls. That requires at least 40,000 retained words and 320 whole comments per block, with compatible dates, lengths and grouping; those counts alone are insufficient. The five-block target requires ten distinct accounts, at least 200,000 retained words and 1,600 final comments, plus adequate auditable reserve. The present frozen pool cannot supply that cohort. No open-ended acquisition or threshold relaxation was used to conceal this deficit.
