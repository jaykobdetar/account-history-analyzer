# Pilot 4 preparation and protection rules

These rules are defined before candidate-pool construction, content audit and all new chronological analysis. The final pool plan binds exact source and code hashes. The acquisition extension preserves the initial 0/1/0 matched-block deficit and authorizes two additional pairs; date/volume gates and the ten-record tolerance remain unchanged.

## Audit-pool construction

Use original physics capacity at its registered best cut plus the two new community pairs at their independently chosen best cuts. Candidate account allocation is fixed before reading original writing for preparation. Allocate globally distinct identities to the pools; final account and cell selection use only metadata and the frozen protection audit. At most two physics accounts and twelve accounts in each new pair enter the audit pool, at most26 total. The exact allocation and deterministic identity ordering are recorded in the preparation plan; no undisclosed account refill follows the audit.

Each account has four community/period cells. Within the same180-day half-bands, take a whole-record prefix ordered by distance from the cut, original timestamp and original ID. Each record must have20–500 retained words. Stop once8,000 words and64 records are both reached, or before exceeding8,500 words or300 records; if the band ends first, retain the available prefix. This buffer supplies only the frozen candidate pool. It is not the scored writing budget. All original source lines are checked against census SHA256 and preserved privately; text, timestamps, IDs and available metadata are copied without rewriting.

Pool limits are100,000 records,2,000,000 words and256MiB of candidate JSONL. Preparation reads at most3,000,000 metadata rows and15,000,000 source rows, with10,000,000,000 declared uncompressed utterance bytes,1800seconds wall and4GiB address space. Failure preserves the attempt and supplies no usable final cohort. Hash-bound sources are the only archives scanned. No style feature or distance is used for construction.

## Frozen protection audit

Run the unchanged pilot3 `audit_candidates.py` wrapper and reviewed pilot2 `leakage_audit.py` engine. The wrapper's version remains its original pilot3 identifier; this is reuse, not a revised analytical method. Bind wrapper, engine, rules, private candidate pool and historical inventory in a `frozen_before_audit` receipt before calling it.

Historical protection includes all original22 supplied protection files used in pilot3 and5,773 unique original records from its60 scored accounts. The original files and protected reserve are unchanged. The reserve enters only automatic exclusion/protection, never score-based design or manual prose inspection. Historical duplicate source rows collapse only when their original content/metadata agree. Separate title nodes continue protecting53 supplied submission titles. Unavailable old bodies and the corpus's unsupplied writing remain disclosed limitations.

The inherited exact relation uses equal nonempty normalized lexical-token segment sequences. Near reuse uses five-token shingles, at least20 retained words, five unique shared shingles, and Jaccard≥0.80 or either directional containment≥0.90. Templates require a15-word phrase across at least three source accounts. Quotation relations use15-word matches from recognized CommonMark blockquotes to retained text. Nothing crosses segment boundaries. All four relations form content components under the frozen code.

Independently enumerate broad shared-shingle candidate pairs, capped at2,000,000, and compare the engine-eligible subset count with the frozen engine's own count. Both the broad count and engine have their existing caps. The wrapper runs under4GiB address space and1800seconds, with network sockets denied. An exhausted audit or missing required grouping is not a passed check.

Purge every candidate member of a content component that touches historical material or multiple candidate cells. Separately apply the same symmetric rule to original thread groups. Cell identity is source account/community/period. Within-cell relations remain logged; intentional later reuse of an anchor does not duplicate the audit input. Historical/new original-ID or account overlap, absent new thread IDs, or conflicting original records fail the audit. Missing historical content remains unknown and prevents an unrestricted independence claim even when the available-content audit completes.

## Post-audit selection and snapshots

For every registered pool account/cell, apply the original nearest-cut5,000-word/40-record stopping rule to surviving records. Reject a half above5,500 words or200 records; no truncation or outside-pool refill. Require all four medians per period within30days, all eight cell word counts within1.10 and all eight record counts within1.25. The original180-day half-bands and20–500 record word bounds remain fixed.

Within each allocated stratum, maximize compatible disjoint blocks up to its registered cap (one original physics, two per new pair), then minimize the exact registered date/word/record imbalance cost. Ties use the sorted source-identity hash order from the original metadata census. Source accounts are disjoint across strata. Fewer available blocks remain an explicit preparation deficit; the scored denominator is frozen before execution. No replacement after analysis or abstention.

Construct every one of the16 planned histories per selected block. Use a uniform declared constructed-account alias for all records in every condition, retaining the real account map privately. Preserve original source IDs, body text, dates, communities, parent and thread fields. Canonical sorting is original timestamp then original ID. The first late record's position is truth only for constructed source-account switches; the analogous continuity junction is a separate diagnostic.

Before scoring, independently check source fidelity, audit membership, budgets, factorial identities, disjoint source cells, account allocation, truth positions and legal whole-record window grids. Publish opaque cell counts, dates, length quantiles and matching diagnostics; publish no source account/record/thread IDs or original writing. Final input and code hashes are bound separately in the scoring registration.
