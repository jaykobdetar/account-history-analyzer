# Pilot 4: matched chronological controls and constructed source-account switches

This is the final method and interpretation protocol before new corpus chronology scores. It consolidates the preserved draft and explicitly registered amendments. It is **not the execution registration** and does not assert that a final cohort has passed preparation. Before any new corpus analysis, a separate `frozen_before_chronological_execution` scoring registration must bind this file, the actual cohort and all planned cases, exact input and code hashes, audit evidence, independent checks, commands, environments and execution limits. Final counts may be below the ceilings below; they must be recorded before scoring.

## Question and scope

Evaluate the frozen full chronological analyzer on source-account continuity controls and constructed source-account switches, each with and without a discussion-community change. The principal distinction is between a candidate occurring anywhere and a candidate locating the constructed source-account junction within the fixed record tolerance. Window resolution, execution availability and localization are reported separately.

Source-account labels are dataset identifiers. They do not establish one human per account, identity, automation, a real account takeover or a naturally occurring writing-style boundary. Continuity means that the constructed halves have the same supplied source account. It does not mean that the person's writing is constant. A community-changing continuity case can contain a real contextual style change despite having no constructed source-account switch.

Inputs are sampled whole comments from two adjacent date bands. “Full analysis” means the complete ordinary analysis pipeline on each constructed snapshot; it does not mean complete real account histories. No paired-comparison substitute, analysis confined to a known seam, added deletion arm, fitted cutoff, method selection, or analyzer tuning is part of this study.

## Score-free date, volume and matching rules

Each pair uses one registered shared UTC cut T. Early records lie in `[T − 180 days, T)`; late records lie in `[T, T + 180 days)`. Timestamps remain original integer UTC seconds. Comments at T belong to late; timestamp ties are not split or retimed to construct a junction.

Eligible construction records have 20–500 frozen retained body words. Titles do not contribute. Within each account/community/period cell, order eligible records by distance from T, original timestamp, then original record ID. Final cell selection takes the whole-record prefix until **both 5,000 retained words and 40 records** are reached. A prefix that exceeds 5,500 words or 200 records is unavailable; it is not shortened, split, skipped around or padded. Surviving records outside the frozen audit pool cannot be added later.

For every A/B account block, there are eight source cells: two accounts × two communities × early/late. Across all eight cells, maximum/minimum retained words must be at most 11/10 and maximum/minimum record count at most 5/4. Within each period, the four account/community median timestamps must span at most 30 days. The median for an even record count is the exact arithmetic midpoint of the two central timestamps. The 500-word construction cap is an experimental input rule; it does not alter production eligibility, windows or features.

Candidate accounts exclude all 57 prior-pilot/protected/private identities and all 60 pilot3-scored identities, 117 distinct exclusions in total. Prior capacity-only exposure is permitted and reported separately. Account allocation to strata, pair dates and candidate ordering are frozen before source writing is assembled for the audit. Final source-account membership is selected only after the registered protection audit, using its surviving records and these metadata gates.

At a candidate cut, compatible edges join eligible source accounts that satisfy the eight-cell gates. In hash-ordered account coordinates, the exact edge cost is the sum over `X/early`, `X/late`, `Y/early` and `Y/late` of:

`abs(median_A − median_B)/(180×86400) + abs(words_A − words_B)/5000 + abs(records_A − records_B)/40`.

Arithmetic is rational. Account order is the full SHA256 of UTF-8 `pilot4-feasibility-identity-order-v1\0` followed by the casefolded account key; `\0` denotes one NUL character. The objectives are, in order, maximum disjoint block cardinality, minimum exact total cost, then the lexicographically first sorted list of hash-ordered account pairs. Across cuts, cardinality and exact cost precede the earliest UTC cut.

The [exact-matching backend registration](EXACT_MATCHING_BACKEND_REGISTRATION.json) permits isolated NetworkX 3.5 weighted blossom for expanded metadata feasibility, preserving these objectives. With E ordered edges and D the least common multiple of all cost denominators, edge rank r receives integer weight `−(cost×D)×2^E + 2^(E−r−1)`, with maximum cardinality enforced first. One scaled cost unit dominates the entire tie-bit range; earlier edge bits dominate all later bits. Verify the pinned wheel, installed files and actual import identity before this metadata search; retain its external wall limit and 4 GiB process ceiling. This study-only dependency is not imported into chronological workers. The original subset-DP feasibility attempt remains unchanged.

## Primary temporal method and window resolution

The fixed primary scope is **pooled comments**, with no community restriction. A community-only stream in a community-changing history contains only part of that history and is not a substitute primary control.

Production primary windows accumulate eligible complete records chronologically and close only after both 1,000 retained words and eight records are reached. A final short remainder remains explicit and unqualified. Original positions refer to the complete canonical supplied snapshot, even if an eligibility exclusion leaves gaps. At least eight qualified windows are required for primary change analysis.

The primary temporal series combines the frozen surface and function-word families, not pilot3's paired character-n4 cosine score. Surface coordinates comprise the ten frozen punctuation rates, uppercase fraction and average word length; function-word coordinates use the frozen vocabulary/rates. Standardization uses the full qualified-window series and population standard deviation. Coordinates missing anywhere or with standard deviation ≤10^-12 are excluded, without imputation. Surviving coordinates receive the frozen feature-count and active-family scaling. The release's L2 segment-cost PELT search minimizes within-segment squared error plus the penalty for internal changes, with primary penalty `1.0×log(N)`, minimum segment length three qualified windows, and jump one. N is the number of qualified windows. Preserve the release's optimizer, numerical behavior and tie handling.

Reconstruct windows independently from the bound metadata and original ordinals, before using candidates. The saved `windows.jsonl` also contains sensitivity windows with the **same stream ID**. Therefore, select primary rows using the exact ordered `window_ids` declared by the selected primary stream summary, including its remainder. Require every declared ID exactly once and verify its stream, chronological order, qualification, record count, word count, original positions and exact record-ID membership against the independent reconstruction. A stream-ID-only filter is invalid because it can mix 500/2,000-word sensitivity windows into the primary grid.

For N ≥8, enumerate all legal single-boundary indices `j=3,…,N−3`. The inclusive split interval is `[last_original_position(window j−1)+1, first_original_position(window j)]`. For N <8, there is no available primary legal grid. This grid uses only qualified whole-record windows and original ordinals; it does not use feature values, optimizer output or a selected candidate.

Full private outputs retain all enabled penalty, window-size, edit/reuse and community sensitivity calculations. They are descriptive diagnostics only. They cannot replace the fixed primary result, define a favorable subgroup or change the tolerance.

## Fixed scoring coordinates, availability and outcomes

The primary tolerance is **τ=10 supplied-record positions, inclusive**. Production bounding-record interval `[a,b]` converts exactly once to inclusive split interval `[a+1,b]`. For truth or diagnostic junction k and interval `[L,U]`, error is `max(L−k, k−U, 0)`. Match if error ≤10. These are supplied canonical record positions—not window indices, archive-wide positions, record-ID suffixes or elapsed days. A candidate interval describes attainable split positions between bounding records; it is not a precisely estimated event time.

Availability follows the frozen account-stream contract. A case is executed for scoring only when the full pipeline completes successfully and the primary native change status is `ok` or `no_measurable_variation`. The latter is an **executed zero-candidate outcome**, normalized to public status `ok` while preserving its native status. Native constant series cannot contain candidates. Other native statuses are abstentions. A whole-pipeline resource failure or other nonzero exit makes the case unavailable even when a partial primary artifact contains boundaries. Preserve missing results, worker failures, undispatched cases and partial outputs with their execution receipts and reasons.

For unavailable cases, candidate occurrence, candidate count and candidate-dependent localization fields are **null**. Partial candidates are not scored. Bound metadata may still support a separately labelled window-grid diagnostic; incomplete saved-window verification must remain explicit. For an executed case with no candidate, occurrence is false and count is zero. A switch match and exact containment are false, while nearest-candidate error and excess error are null; absence of a candidate is not zero localization error.

For every planned case report native/full-pipeline status, availability and reasons. For every executed case report any primary candidate anywhere and the candidate count. For each constructed switch, separately report:

- Whether any candidate matches k within 10 positions and whether any interval contains k exactly (zero tolerance, descriptive).
- Nearest candidate interval and its record error; ties use the earliest interval. With one truth, at most one candidate is matched; retain the count of additional unmatched candidates.
- Best possible interval error on the legal grid, whether the 10-position criterion is attainable, and whether exact containment is attainable.
- Candidate error minus best-grid error when both exist. A grid-unattainable switch remains a failure under τ=10 even if the chosen candidate reaches the best available interval.

For continuity cases, switch-localization fields remain null. Candidate alignment with the constructed sampling junction uses the same arithmetic in a separately named control-junction diagnostic. It is not positive boundary truth and does not establish a false positive for human authorship.

Distinguish insufficient windows/no legal grid, an adequate grid with no candidate, a candidate away from the junction, and a candidate locating it. Grid attainability is a separate attribute, not a way to discard failures or expand tolerance. Report qualified/remainder counts, legal boundary intervals and spacing, possible split-position counts, neighboring window word/record sizes, windows straddling the construction junction, source/cell date summaries and the timestamp brackets around junctions and intervals. Calendar gaps and record-position resolution are different quantities; neither supplies a confidence interval.

## Descriptive aggregation and matched changes

Retain every one of the 16 planned cases per selected block, including unavailable cases. Report preparation deficits, selected and planned blocks, and available/unavailable case counts separately for all three registered strata. A stratum with no selected block contributes no rate and remains visible as a deficit; it is not assigned a zero success rate. Also report status and reason-code counts.

Within each condition and block, give each of its four anchors equal weight. A complete-block mean requires all four values for that metric. Additionally report the available-anchor mean with its explicit available/planned count; it is conditional on observed values. Across blocks, report equally weighted complete-block and available-block means with their respective block counts, overall and by represented stratum. Overall weighting is by block, not an implicit equal-stratum macro. Candidate-error means condition on a candidate existing, and their smaller denominators must be visible.

Matched contrasts use the same anchor in both conditions and only common observed outcomes. Compute switch minus continuity occurrence separately within unchanged and changed communities; community-changed minus same-community occurrence separately for continuity and switches; community-changed minus same-community switch localization; and the corresponding control-junction alignment difference. Also report the occurrence interaction `(switch−continuity with community change) − (switch−continuity without community change)` on anchors with all four outcomes observed. Average anchor differences within a block first; complete-block contrast means require all four common anchors. Report available-anchor/block counterparts and missingness explicitly.

With at most five account blocks and intentionally related cases, this protocol specifies **descriptive results only**. It does not specify a bootstrap, IID confidence interval, p-value, significance threshold, population accuracy claim or success target. Matched differences do not identify a causal effect of community or source account. No post-score replacement, refill, cut change, tolerance change or method substitution is permitted.
