For each of the four early anchors, append in turn (1)same account/same community late, (2)different account/same community late, (3)same account/different community late and (4)different account/different community late. This is16 related streams per block, four per condition. The same early cell is reused across its four planned controls; no individual stream may reuse an original record on both sides. Blocks and strata must not share account identities.

Preserve original body text, original source IDs, creation timestamps and community metadata. Assign every record in one constructed stream the same declared study account alias, for both continuity and switch cases, because the snapshot loader requires one account. Preserve actual source-account mappings privately. Labels concern supplied source account transitions, not observed takeovers. Empty truth arrays for continuity cases mean no constructed source-account reassignment, not an assertion that human writing never changed.

The truth split k is the first right-half record's zero-based position after complete canonical sorting by original timestamp and ID. Left timestamps are strictly beforeT and right timestamps are at/afterT. No timestamp synthesis, retiming, splitting, paraphrasing or padding. Controls retain the same planned junction k as a separate diagnostic, without turning it into positive switch truth. No additional deletion arms are part of this request.

## Frozen scoring tolerance and separate outcomes

Primary tolerance is **10 canonical supplied-record positions**, inclusive, fixed before any chronological run. A production interval[last_left,first_right] maps to the inclusive split interval[last_left+1,first_right]. Interval-location error for k is max(L−k,k−U,0). Exact interval containment (zero tolerance) and continuous location errors are descriptive diagnostics; they do not replace the10-record endpoint after inspection.

Report native execution/resource status and primary availability first. For executed streams, report any primary candidate anywhere and the candidate count. For constructed switches, separately report whether a candidate matches the known split within10, nearest interval-location error, exact containment, and additional off-target candidates. For continuity controls, report occurrence/count under the source-account continuity proxy, plus candidate alignment with the artificial sampling junction as a separate diagnostic. Such candidates are not established false positives for human authorship. Unavailable streams receive null occurrence/count/localization, not reassuring zeros. Qualified `no_measurable_variation` is an executed empty-candidate outcome.

Independently reconstruct unchanged1,000-word/eight-record whole-comment windows in complete supplied-record coordinates. Fewer than eight qualified windows makes primary search unavailable; minimum segment length remains three qualified windows. At N≥8, legal single splits are j=3,…,N−3, mapped to[last_position(window j−1)+1,first_position(window j)]. Compute the nearest attainable interval error and whether10 records is attainable without consulting the optimizer. Never snap construction truth to a legal grid edge.

Keep grid-unattainable, adequate-no-candidate, candidate-off-target, and localized cases separate. Report candidate error minus best-grid error, neighboring window word/record sizes, grid spacing, candidate interval width, temporal bracketing gaps, qualified/remainder counts, and any window crossing the construction seam. Wide uncertainty intervals are not extra scoring tolerance. Record and calendar-time resolution are distinct.

All four conditions retain their planned denominators. Anchor repetitions and16 related streams are not independent subjects; summarize within block first and disclose independent block counts. Report descriptive condition rates and matched condition changes on common executed anchors/blocks, alongside their coverage. No fitted cutoff, best-method substitution, IID confidence interval, accuracy target or repeated data selection until success.
