## Paired-text sample and separation

The intended cohort has 36 additional account slots: 12 development, 12 evaluation and 12 untouched confirmation, with four home-community slots in each partition. All twelve exposed pilot accounts, placeholder accounts and AutoModerator are excluded. Other account automation/shared use is unknown. Global casefolded source account identity, including cross-community identity, defines the author grouping; account names/hashes never enter feature calculations.

The exact pre-shortlist/rank, partition salts and calendar rules are machine-readable in `plan.json`. A metadata shortlist avoids expensive processing of the whole corpus. Equal partition quotas and identical selection rules give each partition comparable opportunities, without claiming equal realized retention. Home community is the largest 2017/2018 raw source-text volume; it is not inferred identity. Eligible retained-word counts determine the selected four accounts per home/partition before any similarity score. The account list freezes before purging, with every exclusion and unfilled slot recorded. No account is replaced after a purge, abstention or unfavorable score.

Two fixed cells use January through September 2017 and the corresponding months of 2018. For the four accounts in a home/partition cell, enumerate the three possible pairings into two disjoint two-account blocks. Minimize the sum of absolute differences of the accounts' early and late median creation timestamps, breaking ties by ordered author hashes. If a median is unavailable, report unavailable matching cost and use the deterministic hash tie rule; never use a fabricated zero timestamp.

For each block's four base cells (Aearly, Alate, Bearly, Blate), fix one shared word budget:

`B = max(1000, min(3000, minimum eligible-word capacity of the four cells))`.

This is a preregistered sample-size adaptation. It equalizes the target within a block while preserving sparse cells as sparse; it does not lower AHAS's default 1,000-word or eight-eligible-record comparison guards. Select whole eligible records by absolute UTC distance from May 16 of the cell's year, then timestamp and original ID, until the budget and eight-record target are met or the source is exhausted. Export in canonical chronology. Record target B, actual eligible words, whole-record overshoot, record count, largest-record share, time endpoints, median and pair time gaps. The shared calendar/target reduces confounding but does not guarantee matched realized dates or identical word counts; those differences remain observations.

Each block contributes two same-account and two crossed different-account pairs. These are account-ID proxy labels, not verified human authorship. The same accounts also define a cross-community condition: early home-community writing versus late writing from the next community in the fixed cycle. This condition has its own shared budget from its four full cells. No cross-community text is invented; missing/undersized units remain empty or insufficient and are counted in coverage. Community membership does not certify exact topic.

The same four omission arms apply to base units: full, fixed hash retention of approximately one half, removal of the chronological middle half, and removal of Cornell records. Surviving source strings and times are unchanged. The base budget is never refilled or lowered after an omission. A community omission can empty a within-community unit; that is an availability observation.

One dataset per predefined home-community/condition/arm/method uses the existing paired-text interface, with both development and evaluation pairs. Its files contain the actual selected subsets, not hidden complete-history feature loads. The scored documents physically omit all confirmation texts, since the current evaluator loads every listed text even when no pair references it. The reserve is allowed only fixed eligibility/provenance/leakage checks; no reserve distance is calculated, no reserve result selects a representation or threshold, and it is not silently used to repair a failing experiment.

## Chronological experiment

The target is six independent source-account splices, two per home community, from twelve additional accounts excluded from both the old pilot and the new paired cohort/reserve. The resource-bounded sampling frame contains complete available three-corpus comment histories of 200–400 records, at least 40,000 present raw codepoints and at least 8,000 eligible retained words. Rank by the fixed chronology hash; apply these volume/provenance exclusions before choosing four per community. No legal grid, change candidate or score selects an account. Pair closest median source times with deterministic ties. Report unfilled slots; never trim a larger account to evade this frame or the production guards.

For each pair, the preregistered cut is the median of the union of actual creation timestamps. The constructed stream contains A's records before that cut and B's records at or after it across their entire available time range. Account metadata is reassigned for construction; source IDs, text and creation times remain intact. This is a controlled source-account splice, not an observed takeover. Each source pair is used once; omission variants are related repetitions of that construction.

The same hash/middle-half/Cornell omissions delete from the full constructed stream without replacement. Record the survivor-to-original ordinal map. The construction truth is the first surviving B record's ordinal in the supplied canonical stream, with original-full ordinal correspondence retained. If one source side disappears or chronology cannot establish a label, report a non-comparable construction; never relabel it unchanged. The main task analyzes whole supplied histories, not snippets centered on the known cut.

Six separate A-source account-ID-continuity proxy controls can use empty construction-truth arrays only with an explicit operational label: no observed source-account reassignment. This never certifies unchanged human authorship. Three preregistered natural histories, one A control per home community, use ordinary `analyze` with unknown authorship truth and no false-positive score. Reusing a history across these descriptions does not create an independent subject.

Audit full source author/thread/document/related components against the paired partitions and reserve. Reject entire candidate histories with known confirmation-group conflicts before selection.
