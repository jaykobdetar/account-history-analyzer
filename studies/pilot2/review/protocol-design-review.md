# Pilot 2 protocol design review

This is a design recommendation, not a frozen preregistration or a scored result. It reads the frozen AHAS 1.0.4 interfaces and the Stage B request. No source, dependency, schema, corpus or score was changed by this review. The original pilot remains preliminary development evidence; none of its inspected results become fresh confirmation data.

## Recommended sampling and separation

Keep the proposed **36 additional account slots: 12 development, 12 evaluation, 12 untouched confirmation**, with four assigned-home-community slots per partition in Cornell, ApplyingToCollege and college. Assign a global Reddit account identity once across all archives; an account cannot occupy different partitions through different community aliases. Home-community assignment is metadata for quota accounting, not an analytical feature or a claim about a person's identity. Freeze the home-assignment and hash tie rule before filling slots. Exclude the first pilot's selected accounts from the new confirmation/evaluation frame, and audit related threads and duplicated records against that earlier material.

Use equal author quotas in the three partitions. Following the revised draft, select the 36 accounts first using minimum eligible-word volume across 2017 January–September and 2018 January–September, with fixed hash ties. Then symmetrically remove every paired candidate record belonging to a thread that spans selected development, evaluation or confirmation accounts. Do not introduce a second arbitrary thread-hash filter. Freeze the selection before this purge and make no iterative account replacements afterward. This avoids asymmetric retention rules but does not guarantee equal realized retention or adequate text. Report counts before and after every filter separately for each community and partition. Do not top up after scoring, after a bad ranking, or after an omission-arm abstention.

Select accounts using pre-score eligibility and matching metadata only. Freeze all selected IDs, exclusions, pair memberships, source/configuration hashes and intended omissions before distances. If a quota cannot be filled, keep the planned slot and its specific unavailable reason in the external inventory. A preregistered target is not permission to invent or replace a missing account after outcomes are known.

`source_document` may honestly contain the **original Reddit comment/post IDs**: these identify actual documents. Namespace them by platform, not by corpus filename, and reject conflicting duplicate IDs across archives. Describe the resulting check as document-level source disjointness. These three education communities share the ConvoKit/Pushshift collection and are related contexts; they are not independent source collections, random Reddit samples, or exact topic controls. Using a shared archive ID as `source_document` would instead make every cross-partition audit overlap and is not the requested document grouping.

Use actual selected thread/root IDs for `thread`, global account IDs for `author`, and genuine baseline-unit/history/component IDs for `related_sample`. Every missingness descendant and reused unit retains its parent relationship. Never generate a fresh unrelated group ID just to make a split pass. Audit all three partitions externally; the native evaluator only compares its development/evaluation pair.

## Whole-record budgets and matched controls

Use the adopted **matched whole-record budget adaptation**: for each condition's two-account block, set one target B = max(1,000, min(3,000, minimum eligible-word capacity of A-early, A-late, B-early and B-late)) from the post-purge inventory. All four baseline units target this same B, leaving the production 1,000-word/eight-eligible-record guards unchanged. A capacity below 1,000 words or eight eligible records remains an abstention; the formula does not manufacture missing text. Eligible words come from the existing frozen preprocessing and records satisfying the existing 20-word condition. Preserve exact source text, timestamps, record boundaries and source statuses. Do not split a long comment, synthesize filler, or infer edits. Corpus English remains an explicit assumption unless independently documented; do not silently recode unknown language as verified English.

The registered plan fixes matching calendar cells: [2017-01-01T00:00:00Z,2017-10-01T00:00:00Z) and [2018-01-01T00:00:00Z,2018-10-01T00:00:00Z), centered on May 16 at 00:00 UTC in each year. Use those same nine-month ranges for all four units in a block. They define sampled source periods, not a guarantee of complete capture. Do not choose a center from distances, candidate boundaries or confirmation outcomes. A shared center does not itself guarantee equal realized medians or time spans; those remain mandatory matching diagnostics.

Within a cell, the adopted adapter ranks the already eligible post-purge candidate records by absolute timestamp distance to the registered center, then actual timestamp and original ID, stopping when eligible words reach the block's fixed B and eligible-record count reaches eight or the cell is exhausted. It exports the selected whole records in canonical chronological order. The final record remains intact. This is a sample from eligible supplied records; unavailable or short intervening comments are outside this paired pool. Write the actual selected subset to its own sampled-snapshot JSONL; the evaluator extracts every row in a source snapshot even when a selector is narrower, so do not point small paired units at massive complete histories.

For every unit record B, eligible words, all retained words, eligible/total record counts, target overshoot, largest-record share, first/last timestamps and median timestamp. Equal comment counts are not equal text volume. A large last-record overshoot stays visible; it is not truncated away. Each omission arm removes records from these same baseline units without recomputing B or replenishing text.

Replace the unconstrained ring with disjoint **two-account matched blocks**. For accounts A and B, use A-early, A-late, B-early and B-late from the same registered time cells. Emit two same-account pairs (A-early/A-late; B-early/B-late) and two crossed different-account pairs (A-early/B-late; B-early/A-late). Thus each unit contributes equally to both labels. Within a four-account community/partition quota, two blocks produce eight pairs from four accounts, not eight independent people.

The adopted paired matching enumerates the three perfect matchings of each four-account stratum and minimizes the summed absolute early/late cell-median timestamp differences, then uses the registered author-hash tie. If any required cell median is missing, cost is null and the lexicographically smallest author-hash matching is selected. There is no hard time-gap or realized word-ratio exclusion. Thus balanced use of the same four units controls label exposure but does not guarantee matched calendar ranges or exact word totals. Report those residual differences without replacing blocks after inspection.

For within-community conditions both sides use that same community. For the revised cross-community condition, each block uses its home community C in the early cell and the next community D in a fixed, explicitly listed cyclic order in the late cell. Use the same four-pair block and the same budget/time rules. Record unsupported or unqualified cross-community slots with empty samples and explicit external reasons; never invent source coverage, silently substitute accounts or reverse direction after inspecting scores. Community context is a coarse control and does not establish shared topic.

## Reuse, templates and quotation audit

Run a reproducible audit on the complete **pre-score candidate material used for selection**, including confirmation candidates, while withholding distance/ranking outputs. Hash and freeze its code, scope and results before scoring. This is fixed preprocessing/provenance inspection, not training on confirmation labels. No corpus-wide completeness claim follows from an audit limited to the selected candidate pool.

The adopted `pilot2_leakage_audit_v1` uses frozen AHAS 1.0.4 preprocessing. Exact matches compare nonempty normalized lexical-token segment sequences, retaining number tokens and segment boundaries. Near comparisons use exact sets of segment-local five-lexical-token shingles, require at least 20 word tokens in each record and at least five shared distinct shingles, and match when Jaccard is at least 80/100 or either directional containment is at least 90/100. Integer cross-products implement these boundaries. The both-record 20-word guard is a named audit adaptation, distinct from production's shorter-record 50-word containment guard. The fixed cap is 2,000,000 unique eligible pairs sharing at least one shingle; observing another pair stops the audit as `not_auditable`, with no actionable purge IDs or components. There is no truncation or cap increase. Exact hashes alone cannot stand in for this step.

Templates are exact contiguous 15-word-token phrases occurring in at least three distinct account keys. Quotation edges connect a recognized CommonMark blockquote inline paragraph's 15-word-token phrase to another record's retained phrase, preserving original line maps. Paragraph boundaries and frozen preprocessing exclusions for code, URLs and mentions are respected; phrases never bridge segments. Template and quote relationships have separate counts and same/cross-partition and same/cross-account incidence diagnostics. These are provenance adaptations outside the production measurement model, with no claimed validation accuracy. Unmarked quotations, short repeated phrases, semantic paraphrases and copyrighted-source attribution remain outside this mechanical check. Preserve original strings privately; public audit summaries contain no source prose and signature references are hashes. An audit flag is not a finding of deception.

Union all exact, near, template and quote edges into transitive components. Component IDs hash their sorted original record IDs and the audit definition. Include honest singleton nodes only after completing the graph; empty retained text never forms a giant empty-match group. Explicit non-present records with null text remain metadata nodes with unknown hidden-content relationships. Missing text without an explicit non-present status blocks the audit. A singleton does not establish independence of unobserved content. Chronological protection requires a joint graph with the complete pre-purge paired pool, rather than comparing previously generated component IDs to new records.

Cross-partition related components must not be hidden. For paired subsets, follow the revised symmetric policy: identify components spanning two or more selected partitions and remove **all** their conflicting candidate records before final budget construction, with every exclusion counted. Compute audit edges over the pre-purge selected candidate pool so earlier removals cannot hide a transitive relationship. Apply a fixed, documented pass rather than outcome-driven iterative replacements. Rebuild units only from the already audited pool; do not introduce unaudited replacement text. If component growth or resource limits make the audit incomplete, mark the affected dimension incomplete/not auditable and block claims of strict confirmation separation. Do not silently drop candidate comparisons, lower audit guards, or assign fake singleton labels to finish.

The final protocol should distinguish source-record overlap, shared threads, shared authors, near-match components, template/quote relationships and common-corpus dependence. Also record within-partition sharing: development/evaluation disjointness does not make crossed pairs or reuse of units independent.

## Confirmation and fixed omissions

Keep confirmation source files and all confirmation `texts`/pairs out of every scored dataset document. `_paired()` loads every declared text, including unreferenced ones. An unreferenced confirmation unit is therefore not a sealed reserve. Store the reserve separately with a frozen inventory and hashes; do not compute its distances, inspect feature contributions or use its outcomes to revise representation, selection, penalties or reporting rules. Opening the reserve ends its untouched status and must be recorded once.

For each method/condition, the registered files separate baseline from three fixed omissions: `hash50` keeps records whose SHA256(omission salt + ':' + original ID) interpreted as an integer has even parity; `middle50` removes canonical indices from floor(n/4), inclusive, to floor(3n/4), exclusive; `drop_Cornell` removes records whose original community is Cornell. Hash parity does not guarantee exactly half in a small unit. For an all-Cornell unit the last omission removes all text; its result is unavailable, not an error to repair. A multi-community natural history may retain other records. Keep pair IDs and surviving original records unchanged, with no replenishment. Record-order deletion is not calendar-time deletion; name it accurately.

The three analytical methods remain retained-prose cosine n=4 **primary**, function-mask cosine n=4 and function-word JS secondary. Every frozen threshold remains null. Keep each method/condition/omission in a separate evaluator document because native outputs do not stratify arbitrary condition fields. Fixed per-community/per-condition batches must stay under 50 MiB of dataset plus unique source files, 10,000 records across distinct canonical snapshots, 1,000 text/stream units and 10,000 pairs per invocation. Batching is operational, not permission to pool correlated results as independent cases.

Report coverage first: planned account/unit/pair slots, exclusion reasons, constructed samples, scored pairs and abstentions per label, partition, method, community condition and omission. Export every individual score and null with its sample-volume context, then complete score distributions and native AUC/AP where both scored labels permit them. Calculable raw distances below guards remain descriptive raw values; they do not turn null evaluation scores into qualified observations. Entirely unsupported conditions retain their planned external denominator even if the native dataset has zero pairs. Do not pool arms or methods as independent people, and do not let a high AUC on a small surviving subset hide lost coverage.

## Chronology, legal grids and unavailable outcomes

Preserve complete supplied natural histories for the chronological task. The paired author/thread/component filters produce sampled subsets and must not be reused under a complete-history label. For strict chronological partitioning, form connected components from complete source-author/thread/related-unit membership before assigning components to partitions. A giant component may make balanced strict separation infeasible. Do not discard inconvenient threads from a complete history or omit known group metadata to force the native audit to pass. Keep such whole-history work explicitly exploratory until a defensible split exists; strict confirmation remains unestablished.

Separate account-continuity proxy controls, unknown-truth natural histories and source-account splices. Unknown truth cannot be encoded faithfully by `truth_boundaries: []`: the native account-stream adapter counts that as known unchanged. Use ordinary `analyze` plus an external unknown-truth inventory for those histories. Even a continuity proxy's empty construction list does not certify stable human authorship, and its candidates are not established false alarms.

For the old four splice cases, independently enumerate the legal grid under frozen 1.0.3/1.0.4 and preserve the original ten-record tolerance and original pass/fail. The supplied best-grid-error values 5, 11, 9 and 9 are hypotheses to verify against actual memberships, not results this design review has run. The case with best legal error 11 still fails a tolerance of ten even if its selected candidate attains that best grid point.

For the new chronological protocol, retain a primary ten-record inclusive tolerance unless a different value is explicitly adopted before scoring. Its unit is a split position in the complete canonical **supplied snapshot**, not an exact date. With N qualified windows, default minimum segment length m=3 and jump=1, legal single-boundary locations are j=m,…,N−m after the minimum-eight-window input gate. Translate each to `[last_left_record_position+1, first_right_record_position]`. For construction split k, error is zero inside that interval, otherwise distance to its nearer endpoint. Grid error is the minimum over this independently enumerated set; feasibility is grid error≤tolerance. An empty/unavailable grid has no finite error, not zero.

Report the actual candidate's interval error, nearest legal grid error, feasibility and optional predeclared excess error separately. Do not use the grid to choose construction cuts, censor hard cases, or change matching tolerance. Preserve candidate-to-truth matching as the existing one-to-one rule. For omission variants, carry a sidecar map from surviving IDs to baseline/source ordinals. Native evaluator coordinates refer to each supplied variant's canonical rows; any baseline-ordinal diagnostic is a separate explicitly named output, not silently substituted native coordinates.

List every planned stream as insufficient input, pipeline resource-limited, adequately executed with zero candidates, off-target candidate, within-tolerance match, or representation-grid infeasible. Some of these descriptors can coexist (for example, an off-target candidate and an infeasible tolerance). Keep coverage denominators over all planned cases; score-location summaries only use their stated executed/matched subsets. Account-continuity and constructed-truth rates remain separate. No independence confidence interval should treat multiple splices from the same authors, crossed pairs or omission arms as independent people.

## Existing-interface boundaries

No schema or numerical-method change is needed for this plan. The extra sampling, grouping, grid and feasibility inventories belong in external protocol/audit sidecars. Existing strict evaluator schemas support only development/evaluation, one method or one tolerance per document, and explicit known boundary arrays; they do not support a confirmation split, unknown truth, condition strata, word-budget provenance, missing planned slots or baseline-coordinate maps. The same canonical snapshot cannot span development/evaluation even with disjoint selectors. Native grouping checks validate supplied IDs, not their provenance, and report missing dimensions as not auditable.

Before many full histories, freeze representative natural-text timing/RAM/output-size probes and their resource ceilings. Run them separately from scientific score summaries, sequentially where possible. Existing input and output limits remain in effect; profiling is not authorization for truncation, downsampling, new algorithms or changed production defaults.
