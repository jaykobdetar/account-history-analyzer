# Pre-score preparation review

This review compares the registered `protocol/plan.json` with the external `scripts/prepare_pairs.py` and `scripts/prepare_streams.py` adapters. It includes the separately recorded actual content-audit checks. It does not calculate style scores or certify natural-person authorship, random sampling, corpus independence or real-world validation. No frozen AHAS package file was changed.

## Paired preparation

The source implements the registered global casefolded account identity, metadata-based home-community assignment, hash partitioning, equal shortlist/selection quotas, eligible-word ranking and no account replacements after purge. Account keys, aliases, labels and source archive names control selection/provenance rather than text features. Placeholder and literal AutoModerator exclusions are explicit; they do not classify other accounts.

The completed content audit is bound to the exact candidate-pool bytes. The finalizer requires every candidate ID, including singleton nodes, to occur in the audited components and removes every member of a cross-partition component. Thread exclusions run separately over the original selected candidate pool and remove all records of cross-partition threads. All surviving sample records therefore come from the same audited population; no unaudited refill is introduced.

For each condition's two-account block, all four baseline cells share the registered B derived from post-purge capacities. The center ordering and timestamp/ID ties match the plan. Whole records remain intact, so overshoot and calendar-range differences are real residual imbalances. The four-pair construction reuses each unit equally in same-account and different-account labels. This balance does not make those pairs independent subjects. Missing or small cells remain unavailable or below guards rather than causing replacement.

All omissions derive from those exact baseline units with unchanged B and no refill: hash parity, the specified middle ordinal interval, or original Cornell community removal. Hash parity is an expected half, not an exact half. Conditions, arms and methods have separate evaluator documents. Confirmation units are written under a separate subtree and never added to scored document text or pair lists. Empty units omit nonexistent source/thread/component arrays; their missing group scope and inadequate text must remain visible.

The registered replay reproduced the exact original candidate bytes. Its additive saved-plan clarification and replay are recorded in `inventory/paired-registered-replay.json`; the completed content audit remains applicable to that byte-identical pool. The paired adapter owner runs emitted-unit and schema preflight separately; this source review does not substitute for those actual tests.

## Chronological preparation

The source excludes all selected paired accounts, including confirmation, before chronology selection. It combines actual account identities across the three archives and preserves complete available comment histories after choosing accounts. Cheap confirmation source/thread overlap rejects entire candidate authors. Eligibility uses the registered count/word guards and hash order, without querying candidate boundaries or legal grids. It selects no replacement author after the joint content audit.

The joint audit includes complete provisional chronological records plus the complete pre-purge paired pool. Any chronological author touching a confirmation component is rejected as a whole; records are not removed to make a natural history appear independent. The actual completed joint graph introduces no new paired cross-partition bridges. Observed chronological thread/component sharing remains reported, so chronology is explicitly exploratory.

One binding guard gap was reported during review: checking only whether protection-pool account keys belong to the selected cohort would admit a truncated pool. `verify_paired_pool()` now requires the supplied pool SHA256 to equal the selected cohort's recorded candidate-pool SHA256 before loading it. The actual preparation had already used the correct, independently verified full pool. This is an adapter integrity guard, not a sampling or analytical-method change; its regression gate is recorded by the owning agent/root.

The chronological median convention is the upper-middle actual timestamp, including the union cut. Closest-pair matching is greedy, with explicit hash ties and orientation; these conventions must be included in the pre-score implementation freeze because the registered prose says only “median” and “closest.” Text, original IDs and creation times survive construction; only account metadata is reassigned. Source-side truth describes a construction, not verified authorship change.

Omission variants retain original source records and separate ordinal maps. A construction with a missing source side has unknown construction truth and uses ordinary analysis instead of silently supplying an empty truth list. Continuity-proxy empty lists mean no observed account-ID reassignment; natural unknown-truth views use ordinary analysis. Post-execution grid diagnostics cannot influence selection because they are imported and calculated only after the scoring freeze and actual execution.

## Limits that remain

Equal account quotas and symmetric rules do not guarantee equal retention or qualification. Paired sampling is conditioned on volume, time cells and observed overlap; its exclusions change the selected population. The three education communities share one source collection and provide coarse context, not independent topic-controlled replications. Corpus English is assumed. Unknown edits, unmarked quotations, unseen material and natural-person identity remain unverified. The actual audit and the planned coverage/abstention inventories must be reported alongside any later metrics.

The native interfaces cannot represent a third confirmation partition, unknown authorship truth, arbitrary condition stratification or baseline-ordinal maps. The adapters therefore use a physically separate reserve and explicit external inventories rather than changing schemas, silently inventing passed group metadata, or relabeling missing outcomes. Related methods, omission arms, crossed pairs and repeated source histories do not increase the number of independent accounts.
