## Construction and limits

The [protocol](protocol/PROTOCOL_V1.md) fixed five source samples per pair: one A/X earlier anchor and four continuations, A/X, B/X, A/Y and B/Y. The same earlier records appear in all four histories. Directions and junction dates were chosen from source metadata before scoring; candidate results and favorable grid positions were never selection criteria. This design does not test every reciprocal account/community direction.

A finite search over 505 community/date combinations produced an exact matching of 32 disjoint pairs; its fixed first 20 entered auditing. The combined pool contained 8,867 original comments. The unchanged content/thread audit purged 857, leaving 8,010 records for the fixed post-audit prefixes and gates. [All 20 availability decisions](inventory/prepared-01/pair-availability.json) and earlier unsuccessful preparation attempts remain preserved.

The selected cohort uses 2,531 distinct comments and 228,387 retained words from 18 source accounts. All 119 protected or previously scored accounts were excluded. Two selected accounts had earlier unscored preparation/audit exposure; sixteen appeared only in earlier eligibility metadata. The cohort covers academic, math, Linux and programming community pairs; no provisional Physics pair passed the final gates. The historical audit still has 17 records with unobservable content. See [source availability](inventory/README.md) for the full scope and prior-exposure accounting.

The unit is a source-account pair: nine pairs, not 36 or 72 independent replications. Account labels do not establish different human authors. The findings describe this bounded constructed cohort; they do not establish population accuracy, real account-takeover detection, or a causal explanation for a change candidate.
