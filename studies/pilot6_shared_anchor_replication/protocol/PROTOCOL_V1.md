# Pilot 6: bounded shared-anchor replication

This protocol is registered before new pair/direction/calendar enumeration, candidate content auditing or chronological scoring. Pilot 5 is accepted as a completed exploratory diagnostic. Its original inputs, registrations, outputs and receipts remain unchanged and do not count as a new replication. Pilot 6 uses frozen AHAS 1.0.4. Subsequent hash registrations bind each executed preparation stage and the final cohort before scoring.

## Fixed source universe and exclusions

Use the ten already available, hash-bound research-corpus archives in five community pairs: AskAcademia/GradSchool, AskPhysics/Physics, math/learnmath, linux/linuxquestions, and programming/learnprogramming. No new communities, platform, source download or adaptive calendar expansion belongs to this milestone. Preserve original source writing, timestamps, IDs, community, thread and parent fields. Source labels are account proxies; corpus-level English is an assumption.

Reuse the complete earlier academic/physics/math eligibility metadata. For the two technical pairs, retain existing eligibility metadata and make one bounded supplement for account/community memberships omitted by the old 80-present-comment-per-community intake prefilter. The five-sample design requires at least 40 comments in each community for any source account, so use 40 in both as a safe necessary intake condition; actual anchor eligibility still requires distinct early and late samples. This changes a preparation prefilter, not an analyzer guard. Reuse the frozen preprocessor and source conversion; retain hashes of original source lines. Exclude the 57 earlier protected/private/prior-pilot accounts, all 60 Pilot 3 scored accounts, and both Pilot 5 accounts before new preprocessing. Existing metadata is filtered to the same 119-account exclusion union. Prior metadata/capacity exposure is disclosed and never counted as unseen source availability.

The old known protection inventory is extended mechanically with the 216 distinct original comments scored in Pilot 5. Protected reserve writing enters automatic content/thread exclusion only. No manual inspection of protected prose or scored feature vectors informs selection.

## Finite metadata enumeration and provisional audit pool

For all five community pairs, enumerate the same 101 monthly UTC cuts, 2010-01-01 through 2018-05-01 inclusive. At each cut T, use early [T−180 days,T) and late [T,T+180 days). An ordered direction A/B, X/Y has five disjoint samples: A/X early, A/X late, B/X late, A/Y late, B/Y late. A and B differ. All four histories share the exact A/X early records.

Reuse Pilot 5 whole-comment construction. Eligible records have 20–500 frozen retained words. Sort nearest the cut first, then original timestamp and ID. Stop when both 5,000 retained words and 40 records are reached; reject a prefix exceeding 5,500 words or 200 records. No comment may be split, padded, skipped to repair a prefix, edited or retimed. Restore original chronological timestamp/ID order for histories.

All five samples must have word max/min ≤11/10 and record max/min ≤5/4. The four late-sample medians must span at most 30 days. Each history must yield at least eight independently reconstructed qualified primary windows. There is no early reciprocal median requirement. No candidate result, style distance, feature variation or favorable legal-grid position enters any selection stage. Legal grid error is not calculated until a direction and cut are fixed for preparation/reporting.

Use exactly the Pilot 5 rational metadata cost: sum of six late-pair absolute median differences/(180×86400), plus ten five-sample pair word differences/5000 and ten record-count differences/40. For each unordered source-account pair across all five strata and all cuts, retain the valid direction/cut with smallest cost; ties use community-pair order above, earliest cut, SHA256 of UTF-8 `pilot6-anchor-order-v1` + NUL + casefolded A then B, then literal X and Y. All enumeration counts and failures remain recorded; account keys and original IDs stay private.

On those unordered-pair edges, reuse the registered isolated exact integer matching backend to maximize global account-disjoint cardinality, then minimize total rational cost, then break ties by the sorted account-hash edge order. This is preparation matching only; it does not alter the AHAS optimizer or worker environment. Sort the resulting matching by edge cost and the same tie tuple and retain its first 20 edges, or every edge if fewer exist, as the fixed provisional audit pool. This truncates one complete matching; it does not claim a separately optimal capped matching. No account appears in two provisional pairs, even across communities or dates. Each edge's A/B direction and cut are fixed before source preparation and are never replaced after auditing or scoring.

## Source preparation, audits and final cohort

For each provisional pair, take buffers for its fixed five source cells using the unchanged Pilot 4 buffer rule: nearest-cut whole comments in the same bands and 20–500-word bounds, stop at both 8,000 words and 64 records, before 8,500 words or 300 records; retain an exhausted shorter buffer. The five buffers are the only refill pool. Bind selected source lines to the census hashes and keep byte-original lines privately. No unused reciprocal early cells are required.

Audit the combined unique pool once with the unchanged Pilot 3 audit wrapper and reviewed Pilot 2 leakage engine, including the extended historical inventory. Preserve the existing exact, near-reuse, template, quotation, component and original-thread rules. Purge every candidate component/thread crossing a source-account/community/period cell or touching historical material. Audit the anchor once, before intentional reuse. Missing historical content remains unknown. Exhausted/incomplete audits do not pass.

Recompute the fixed five prefixes from each pair's audited survivors, at the same direction and cut, and apply the same Pilot 5 gates and cost. Rank surviving eligible provisional pairs by final cost and the same deterministic tie tuple. The first ten, or every eligible pair if fewer, form the final replication cohort. Freeze this cohort and all four histories per pair before any analyzer scoring. Keep audit-ineligible pairs and eligible pairs outside the ten-pair ceiling visible with explicit unscored reasons. Do not refill from outside the registered buffers, replace directions/cuts after auditing, or add accounts after outcomes.

## Frozen analyses and required outcomes

Each retained pair produces, in order: continuity/same community, constructed account switch/same community, continuity/changed community, and both switches. Only `account_id` is replaced by one uniform opaque alias per constructed history. Production AHAS 1.0.4 full `analyze`, all ordinary modules/sensitivities and all production guards remain unchanged. The primary remains pooled-comments surface/function-word chronology with the release's 1,000-word/eight-record windows, minimum segment length three and primary log(N) penalty. No classifier, optimizer change, new platform or alternative sensitivity substitution is permitted.

The junction k is the number of shared anchor records, never snapped to a window grid. Keep the inclusive ten-record tolerance τ=10. Reuse the existing interval conversion [a,b] bounding records → [a+1,b] split positions, distance arithmetic and primary-window verification. Report every candidate interval, its distance to k, candidate occurrence/count, nearest candidate distance, exact containment, match within ten, nearest attainable legal-grid error and excess error. Legal splits follow qualified windows j=3,…,N−3 for N≥8; timestamp/record resolution remains separate from observed localization. Continuity junction alignment is descriptive, not positive source-switch truth.

Report all four outcomes together for each pair, including full execution/native status, reasons, every interval and operating cost. Successful `no_measurable_variation` is executed zero-candidate behavior. Failed/unavailable/not-dispatched analyses have null detection/localization outputs; metadata resolution is separately labeled. Never interpret an unavailable result as zero. All unfavorable outcomes remain in the denominator and published aggregate record.

## Stopping, resources and replay

One source universe, one finite calendar enumeration, one provisional pool of at most 20 account-disjoint pairs, one combined audit, and one final cohort of at most ten pairs. Stop when all registered histories/replays have been attempted or the declared resource limit prevents further dispatch. A shortfall is a result for this bounded design. No score-dependent acquisition, refill or stopping is permitted. Technical failures retain their receipts; only a documented pre-score correction of execution plumbing may be attempted on the same frozen inputs, without broadening the scientific rules.

Supplement intake: 3,600 seconds, 4 GiB address space, 15 million source rows, 16 GiB declared uncompressed source, two million new preprocessing calls, 768 MiB metadata and 1 GiB private output. Metadata enumeration: 1,800 seconds, 4 GiB, three million metadata rows/1 GiB input, two million directional evaluations and 256 MiB private enumeration output. Preparation: 1,800 seconds, 4 GiB, 25 million source rows/16 GiB uncompressed input, 100,000 candidate records, two million candidate words and 256 MiB private output. Audit retains its existing 1,800 seconds, 4 GiB and two-million broad shared-shingle-pair ceiling; no ceiling is relaxed after seeing a failure.

Reuse the unchanged Pilot 5 four-history execution and verification adapter independently for each retained pair. Each pair uses two parallel workers, 600 seconds and 4 GiB per case, 630-second outer cleanup guard, 3,600-second dispatch ceiling and 8 GiB original-plus-replay artifact ceiling. Across the milestone, enforce 14,400 seconds from first dispatch and 12 GiB original-plus-replay artifacts; reserve a full pair's worst-case eight ×512 MiB before dispatch. Preserve explicit not-dispatched slots if a global ceiling stops work.

Originals use hash seed 0/UTC; all four histories per pair replay with relocated byte-identical inputs, hash seed 73129/Pacific-Honolulu and the same installed package. Compare all canonical artifacts, excluding exactly ingest_receipt.json and run_receipt.json. Replays are verification observations, not additional account pairs. Independently verify selection, original-source fidelity, all saved primary windows and interval/grid arithmetic. Hash-check prior study preservation before and after work.

The target is ten NEW account-disjoint pairs, forty related main histories and forty replays, not eighty independent replications. Report actual denominators separately, including any pre-score shortfall. Accounts need not represent different people; shared community context and unobserved reuse can remain. This is descriptive replication evidence, with no population-accuracy, verified-authorship or real-takeover claim. Pilot 5 may support a regression check but is excluded from all new-pair counts. Only sanitized protocols, aggregate results and receipts are published; original writing, maps and full source-containing analyzer artifacts remain protected.
