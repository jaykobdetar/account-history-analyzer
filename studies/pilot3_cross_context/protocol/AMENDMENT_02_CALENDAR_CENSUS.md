# Score-free amendment 02: exhaustive shared UTC calendar cuts

The independently verified expanded census supports 48 account slots/24 blocks
under the four predeclared date schemes. AskAcademia/GradSchool has 36 four-cell
accounts and AskPhysics/Physics has 32; the next largest pair has nine. Yet the
weaker all-history necessary bound can support three 20-account quotas. No style
scores have been computed, and the first two censuses remain preserved failures
under their original rules.

Before acquiring the separately researched mathematics pair, exhaust the date
question using already generated, hash-bound eligibility metadata. This amendment
does not reread corpus writing or run any preprocessing. Examine every possible
shared UTC midnight cut strictly between 2008-01-01 and 2018-11-01. Early is
`[2008-01-01, cut)`; late is `[cut, 2018-11-01)`. The same cut applies to both
communities and every account in one stratum. Actual original timestamps and
whole records remain unchanged.

For each account/community, find the earliest original timestamp E at which
the chronological prefix reaches both 2,000 eligible words and eight records,
and the latest timestamp L at which a reverse suffix reaches both. A cut t is
valid iff E < t <= L. Convert this to inclusive integer UTC-day boundaries, then
intersect the intervals for the two communities. Account pair feasibility is
therefore an interval, and an exact event sweep measures the number of accounts
available at each shared midnight. Timestamp ties enter a side together; no
record is split or placed on both sides.

For each community pair choose the cut with greatest four-cell account count,
then smallest absolute difference between early and late calendar duration,
then earliest UTC cut. Use the existing exact three-stratum selector on those
account sets, retaining all 28 pair capacities and chosen dates. Maximum per-pair
capacity is exact over the stated full-span midnight-cut family; this does not
prove feasibility/infeasibility of arbitrary separately bounded or gapped periods,
nor global optimality over every combination of nonmaximal per-pair cuts.

Resource ceiling: at most 1,000,000 private eligibility metadata rows, 28 community
pairs, 4 GiB address space, five minutes and 100 MiB additional metadata output.
Zero corpus downloads, zero original-prose reads, zero preprocessing calls and
zero distances are allowed in this refinement. Use the same required exclusions
and 2,000-word/eight-record targets. Retain prior capacity-only exposure flags.

If three disjoint 20-account strata are feasible, freeze the resulting strata
and dates for Gate B. Otherwise preserve the deficit and consider only the two
previously named mathematics archives under an explicit separately bounded
acquisition amendment. No smaller study or lower guard is implicitly approved.
