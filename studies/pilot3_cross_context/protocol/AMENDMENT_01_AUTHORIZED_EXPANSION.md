# Score-free amendment 01: bounded authorized source expansion

The first census failed Gate A: four possible blocks rather than 30 under the
registered calendar grid, with zero Cornell/college capacity even in the necessary
all-history word/record bound. No style distances were computed. The complete
first census, its plan, code identity, command and outputs are retained.

The user subsequently explicitly approved sourcing additional datasets to run
the required tests. This amendment uses that authorization for five named
existing Cornell-hosted ConvoKit archives: AskAcademia, GradSchool, PhD, Physics,
and AskPhysics. It authorizes no Reddit collector or open-ended acquisition.
The official listed total is 284,210,124 compressed bytes. The acquisition ceiling
is 300,000,000 bytes and 15 minutes, with exact individual file sizes checked.
No corpus redistribution license is inferred; raw archives remain private.

Academic-career, graduate-study and doctoral-study communities have related
audiences. Physics and physics-question communities provide another plausible
overlap with different discussion purposes. Current community descriptions are
only an acquisition rationale, not verified historical topic or participant
labels. Actual source records and overlap must be counted.

After archive hashes are observed, bind a new score-free census plan before any
preprocessing of the expanded frame. Inspect at most eight local archives in two
passes, no more than 6,000,000 rows/pass or 4,000,000,000 uncompressed bytes/pass;
preprocess at most 1,000,000 records; cap address space at 4 GiB, wall time at
30 minutes and private output at 1 GiB. Failure of a bound remains incomplete.

Retain the original four calendar schemes, eligibility guards, mandatory
exclusions and whole-record requirements. Consider every community pair from
the eight archived communities. For each pair choose dates using its score-free
four-cell capacity, then the original duration/tie rules. Select three candidate
strata using only capacity: first find triples supporting 20 disjoint accounts
per stratum by exact Hall feasibility. Among feasible triples prefer greater
minimum per-pair capacity, then greater summed capacity, then alphabetic pair
names. If no triple supports the target, report all pair capacities and choose
the best complete-block quota triple by total blocks, balanced quota vector,
minimum raw pair capacity, summed raw pair capacity and alphabetic pair names.
The selected triple is a feasibility proposal, not a final cohort or a passed
leakage audit. No score, feature profile, appealing writing example or boundary
location informs these choices.

Gate B and scoring remain conditional. User acquisition approval does not waive
the full target, the contamination audits, the protected reserve, registration,
resource ceilings, or the unchanged production guards.
